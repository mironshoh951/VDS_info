photographs, downloaded from Wikimedia Commons under licences that permit
reuse with attribution. Nothing here is claimed to be VDS Dental's own
premises — captions describe the real, generic subject ("a modern dental
clinic", "dental equipment") rather than inventing a department, which is
why `prisma/seed/60-media.ts` writes the credit line below into every
